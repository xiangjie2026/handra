常见问题

我的任务存在哪里？会丢吗？
存在你的 iPhone 本机，不上传服务器。只要不删除 App、不抹掉设备，数据就会保留。删除 App 会同时删除数据，请先导出备份。
换手机怎么带走数据？
用系统「传输或还原 iPhone」从旧机备份恢复，或在 App 内导出 Markdown / JSON 留档。v1 不做账号与云同步。
提醒没有响？
依次检查：系统「设置 → 通知 → 手边」是否允许；App 内该任务是否设了提醒；是否开启了专注模式或静音。通知在本机调度，不依赖网络。
小组件不刷新？
先在 App 里完成或新增一项，系统会触发刷新；也可长按小组件选择「刷新」。低电量模式下系统会降低刷新频率。
怎么删除全部数据？
在 App 内逐项删除，或直接卸载 App。卸载后本机数据一并删除；系统备份中的副本请在 iCloud / 电脑备份中管理。
会收费吗？有内购吗？
v1 完全免费，无内购、无订阅、无广告。所有功能开箱即用。
支持 iPad 或 Apple Watch 吗？
v1 只支持 iPhone 竖屏，iOS 17 及以上。iPad 与 Apple Watch 不在本版本范围。

FAQ

Where is my data stored?
On your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded. Data stays unless you delete the app or erase the device. Deleting the app deletes its data, so export first if you need a copy.
How do I move data to a new phone?
Restore the new iPhone from a backup of the old one, or export Markdown / JSON from the app. v1 has no account or cloud sync.
Why didn’t my reminder fire?
Check Settings → Notifications → Handra, confirm the task has a reminder, and check Focus or silent mode. Notifications are scheduled locally and do not need the network.
Why isn’t the widget updating?
Completing or adding a task in the app triggers a refresh; you can also long-press the widget and choose Refresh. Low Power Mode reduces refresh frequency.
How do I delete all data?
Delete items in the app, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes local data; backup copies are managed in iCloud or computer backups.
Is it free? Any purchases?
Version 1.0 is free, with no subscriptions, in-app purchases, or ads.
iPad or Apple Watch support?
v1 supports iPhone in portrait only, iOS 17 or later. iPad and Apple Watch are out of scope for this version.
